# Refund and Cancellation Policy (Draft)

DRAFT - NOT LEGAL ADVICE - LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED

This draft is a proposal only. The refund window, grace period, and every other figure below need a Kartikey decision and a lawyer confirmation before they are published or promised. No paid plan exists yet, so no refund has been paid under this policy.

## 1. Cancellation (proposal)

- A customer may cancel a paid plan at any time, in the app or by contacting [CONTACT EMAIL - NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION], once that route is built.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle. The customer keeps access until then, unless the account is suspended for breach of the Terms or Acceptable Use Policy.
- On cancellation, the workspace is downgraded to Free Pilot, not deleted immediately. Data retention after downgrade is proposed as at least 30 days, then the Privacy Policy deletion rule applies. Both need lawyer confirmation.

## 2. Refund window (proposal, needs Kartikey decision and lawyer confirmation)

- Proposed: a full refund of the first subscription payment, if requested within 7 days of that first payment, and if the workspace has not used more than the Free Pilot allowance in that period, as a proposal to be confirmed.
- Proposed: no refund for provider cost or overage already consumed, because that cost has already been incurred with an AI provider.
- Proposed: no partial-month refund after the 7-day window, and no refund for a renewal that the customer did not cancel before the renewal date, subject to the failed-payment grace rule below.
- Proposed: a refund, if granted, is paid to the original payment method, through the same payment provider, within the provider's normal refund timeline, which Orbely does not control. Orbely will confirm the refund in writing once the provider confirms it.
- These are proposals only. A lawyer must confirm whether a 7-day window, a usage cap, and a no-partial-refund rule are lawful and fair for Indian consumers and for business customers, and whether different rules are needed for consumers versus businesses.

## 3. Failed payment and grace (proposal)

- On a failed renewal, the proposal is 7 days grace with full access, then downgrade to Free Pilot, not deletion. See pricing-billing-suggestion.md, section 7, and the Terms draft, section 5.
- A customer may update its payment method during grace, and the renewal will be retried as the payment provider allows, subject to RBI mandate rules for Indian recurring payments, which a lawyer and Razorpay must confirm.

## 4. How to ask for a refund or cancellation

- Route: [IN-APP CANCELLATION AND REFUND FORM / CONTACT EMAIL - NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION AND PRODUCT BUILD]. Do not publish a route until it is built.
- What to include: workspace name, plan, billing period, payment reference, and reason. Orbely will acknowledge the request, verify the payer and workspace, and answer within [RESPONSE TIME - NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION AND LAWYER CONFIRMATION].
- Disputes and chargebacks: a customer that files a chargeback instead of asking Orbely for a refund may have its workspace suspended pending the dispute outcome, as a proposal, subject to lawyer confirmation. Chargeback fees charged by a payment provider are not refunded by Orbely under this proposal, subject to lawyer confirmation.

## 5. Taxes on refunds

- A refund, if granted, will be for the amount paid, with tax handled as the payment provider and a CA confirm. Orbely will issue a credit note or revised invoice where required, as a CA confirms. This pack does not state a tax treatment for refunds as a verified fact.
