# Acceptable Use Policy (Draft)

DRAFT - NOT LEGAL ADVICE - LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED

This draft must not be published as a final policy until a lawyer reviews it. It applies to every Orbely user, workspace, agent, task, and approval.

## 1. General rule

Use Orbely lawfully, honestly, and only for work you are entitled to do. Do not use Orbely, or an agent running through Orbely, to harm others, break the law, deceive people, or bypass Orbely's own controls.

## 2. Prohibited uses

Do not use Orbely to:

- Break any applicable law, including Indian law, or to help someone else break the law.
- Create, share, or distribute unlawful content, including content that infringes copyright or trademark, defames, harasses, threatens, or discriminates.
- Commit fraud, impersonation, phishing, spam, or deceptive marketing, including fake proof reports, fake case studies, or fake client results.
- Produce, publish, or send content that Orbely's worker does not actually support. The worker records output only and does not spend, publish, or send, so do not present Orbely output as if Orbely had sent or published it.
- Access, or try to access, a workspace, account, agent, or execution you do not belong to, or bypass workspace scoping, budgets, approval flows, or rate limits.
- Upload malware, probe for vulnerabilities without permission, scrape Orbely at a rate that harms the service, or reverse-engineer Orbely to extract keys, secrets, or other customers' data.
- Put secrets, passwords, private keys, or other customers' confidential data into tasks, prompts, or agent instructions, except through Orbely's intended key-reference system, if any.
- Process children's personal data, highly sensitive personal data, or client-confidential data through an AI provider without checking the provider's terms and your own legal duties first.
- Use Orbely to generate content that misleads a client about who did the work, whether a human verified it, or what a proof report actually proves.

## 3. Human verification is mandatory

- Every agent output must be human-verified before it is used, published, sent, or billed to a client. A proof report does not remove that duty.
- A customer that bills a client using a Orbely proof report is responsible for the accuracy of what it bills, and for telling its client, truthfully, what was verified and what was not.

## 4. Enforcement

- Proposal, needs lawyer confirmation: Orbely may warn, suspend, limit, or close an account, workspace, or agent that breaches this policy, after notice where practicable, except where immediate action is needed to prevent harm, abuse, or a security risk.
- Content that breaches this policy may be removed or disabled, subject to the notice and appeal process a lawyer confirms.
- Repeated or serious breaches may lead to permanent closure, and to a report to authorities where required by law, after a lawyer review where practicable.
