## 2. What Orbely does, and does not do

- Orbely lets a team leader create a workspace, invite members, connect agents, create tasks, approve payloads, and view proof reports, usage, budgets, and handoffs.
- Agents produce output. A human must verify and approve work before it is relied on. A task that completes moves to in review, never straight to done, by design.
- The worker records output only. It does not spend money, publish content, or send messages on a customer's behalf, unless a future, separately announced feature says otherwise.
- Orbely does not guarantee that AI output is accurate, complete, lawful, or fit for a particular purpose. AI output can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date. Do not rely on it without human review.
- Email and phone invites are not sent by Orbely today. Invites are by link or invite code only, until a provider is built and announced.
- Proof reports are a record of work done and verified in Orbely. They are not a guarantee to a third party, a certification, or legal advice. An agency that bills a client using a proof report is responsible for its own billing and for what it tells its client.

## 3. Accounts, workspaces, and roles

- The customer is responsible for keeping account credentials safe, and for who it invites to a workspace.
- Workspace leaders can approve payloads, set budgets, and manage members in their workspace. Members can only see workspaces they belong to, subject to Orbely's access rules.
- A leader pressing Run on queued executions processes only queued executions for that workspace, in the current code. The older global worker endpoint, if used by a trusted server-side worker, is a separate path and must be described accurately by a lawyer before launch.
- Orbely may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms, the Acceptable Use Policy, or the law, after notice where practicable, except where immediate suspension is needed to prevent harm, abuse, or a security risk. Suspension and notice rules need lawyer confirmation.

## 4. Customer content and AI output

- Proposal, needs lawyer confirmation: the customer retains its rights in its own inputs, tasks, and workspace content. Orbely does not claim ownership of a customer's content, or of AI output produced for that customer, solely because it was produced through Orbely.
- Orbely may need a limited licence to host, process, display, and back up that content, solely to run the service, and to send it to the AI provider the customer chose for that agent, for example Groq, to produce output. That licence, its scope, and its end on deletion, need lawyer confirmation.
- AI output may not be copyrightable in every jurisdiction, and may reproduce material from training data. The customer is responsible for checking output before use, publication, or billing, and for its own intellectual property and confidentiality duties to its clients.
- The customer must not put secrets, passwords, or other credentials into tasks, prompts, or agent instructions, except through Orbely's intended key-reference system, if any. Orbely stores key references only, not plaintext provider keys, in agent credentials, by design.
