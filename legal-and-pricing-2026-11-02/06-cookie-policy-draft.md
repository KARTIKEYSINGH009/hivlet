# Cookie Policy (Draft)

DRAFT - NOT LEGAL ADVICE - LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED

This draft must not be published as a final cookie policy until a lawyer reviews it, and until the actual cookies used by Orbely, Supabase Auth, and Vercel are listed accurately from a live check.

## 1. What cookies Orbely uses, as a proposal to be verified live

- Strictly necessary cookies: to sign a user in, keep a session, and protect against abuse. These are needed for Orbely to work, and cannot be turned off in the app without breaking sign-in.
- Authentication cookies: set by Supabase Auth, to keep a user signed in across pages. Exact names, duration, and purpose must be listed from a live check before publication. Do not copy a cookie list from another product.
- Hosting and security cookies: set by Vercel, for hosting, load balancing, and abuse prevention, as applicable. Exact names and duration must be listed from a live check before publication.
- Preferences: if Orbely later stores a theme, language, or workspace preference in a cookie or local storage, that must be added here before it is used.
- Analytics and advertising: Orbely does not use advertising cookies today, as a proposal, and must not add analytics or advertising cookies without updating this policy, adding consent where a lawyer says it is needed, and listing the vendor.

## 2. Consent and control

- Proposal, needs lawyer confirmation: strictly necessary cookies do not need consent to work, but must still be listed here. Any non-essential cookie, including analytics, needs consent before it is set, as a lawyer confirms under Indian law and, for international users, under their local law.
- A user can delete or block cookies in their browser, but Orbely may not work correctly, especially sign-in, if necessary cookies are blocked. That warning must be kept in a final policy, as a lawyer confirms.
- Cookie consent tool: [NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION AND PRODUCT BUILD]. Do not promise a cookie banner or a consent tool until it is built and tested.

## 3. Third parties

- Supabase and Vercel may set their own cookies as part of hosting and auth. Their own policies apply to those cookies, and Orbely's policy must link to them accurately, once a lawyer confirms the links.
- AI providers, for example Groq, do not set cookies in a user's browser through Orbely today, as a proposal to be verified live. Do not claim that as a fact until a live check confirms it.
