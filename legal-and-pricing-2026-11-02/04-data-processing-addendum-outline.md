# Data Processing Addendum (Outline, Draft)

DRAFT - NOT LEGAL ADVICE - LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED

This is an outline only, not a signed DPA. A lawyer must turn it into a final DPA before Orbely signs one with any business customer, especially an agency that processes its clients' personal data through Orbely.

## 1. Parties and roles (proposal, needs lawyer confirmation)

- Customer: the business that creates a workspace and invites members, acting as Data Fiduciary for its own clients' personal data, or as a processor for its clients, as its own contracts say.
- Orbely: [COMPANY NAME - NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION], acting as Data Processor for the customer, and as Data Fiduciary for its own account and billing data, as a lawyer confirms. The dual role must be stated clearly in a final DPA, not assumed.
- This outline does not decide whether Orbely is a processor, a fiduciary, or both, for any given data set. That is a lawyer question.

## 2. Subject matter and data (to be filled in, needs Kartikey decision and lawyer confirmation)

- Subject: processing of workspace, task, agent, approval, execution, and proof data, to run Orbely for the customer.
- Personal data categories: account email and auth data, workspace membership and roles, task and agent content a customer puts into Orbely, execution inputs and outputs, usage and cost data.
- Data subjects: the customer's team members, and any person whose personal data the customer puts into a task. The customer must not put a third person's personal data into Orbely without a lawful basis and, where needed, consent.
- Duration: while the workspace exists and the customer uses Orbely, plus the retention period in the Privacy Policy draft, as a lawyer confirms.

## 3. Processor duties (draft, needs lawyer confirmation)

- Process only on the customer's documented instructions, and only for the purposes in the Privacy Policy draft, unless required by law.
- Keep personal data confidential, and make sure staff and contractors who access it are bound by confidentiality.
- Use reasonable security safeguards, as described in 07-security-data-handling-note.md, and help the customer with data principal rights requests, breach notice, and impact assessments, as a lawyer defines the scope and timelines.
- Do not engage a sub-processor without notice to the customer, as a proposal, at least 30 days before the change, and with a right to object, as a lawyer confirms. Sub-processors today, as a factual list, include Vercel, Supabase, and the AI provider chosen for an agent, for example Groq. A payment provider is added only when paid plans are built.

## 4. Sub-processors, transfers, and security (needs lawyer confirmation)

- Sub-processor list, change notice, and objection route: [NEEDS KARTIKEY DECISION AND LAWYER CONFIRMATION].
- International transfers: safeguards and transfer mechanism, [NEEDS LAWYER CONFIRMATION]. Do not promise India-only residency.
- Security measures: encryption, access control, workspace scoping, append-only audit logs, server-side keys only, as in 07-security-data-handling-note.md. No security certification is claimed in this outline.
- Breach notice: notify the customer without delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach, with the details in the Privacy Policy draft, subject to lawyer confirmation of recipients and timelines.

## 5. Return and deletion (needs lawyer confirmation)

- On termination, return or delete the customer's personal data as the customer instructs, except for records Orbely must keep for tax, legal, or fraud reasons, for a period a lawyer confirms.
- Backup deletion on the backup cycle, as a lawyer confirms, must be stated in a final DPA, not assumed.

## 6. What this outline does not do

- It does not create a signed DPA, a GDPR Article 28 agreement, or a DPDP-compliant processor contract by itself.
- It does not cover a customer's own duties to its clients, its own consent, or its own AI-output accuracy duties. Those remain the customer's, and a lawyer must say so in a final DPA.
- It does not list Groq's or any other provider's retention, training, or sub-processor terms, because those are unverified in this pack and must be checked before signing.
