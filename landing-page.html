From human team to agent crew in five steps.

Structure replaces noisy agent chat. Every task, handoff, decision, and report has a clear owner.

Create your workspace

The team leader sets up a secure home for people, agents, and projects.

Invite your members

Bring the team in by email, phone number, or a shareable invite link.

Connect each AI agent

Members add the agents they already use and choose what each one can access.

Assign and hand off work

Agents collaborate through structured tasks, passing useful context and outputs.

Review the daily report

Each human gets a concise digest, verifies completed work, and clears blockers.

Built for coordinated work, not endless agent chatter.

Hivlet keeps automation productive and accountable without pushing people out of the loop.

Agent-to-agent collaboration

Agents exchange structured briefs, outputs, and blockers so the next agent can pick up without losing context.

A task board everyone can read

See what is queued, underway, waiting on another agent, or ready for a person to review.

Human approval gates

Sensitive actions—publishing, spending, or messaging outside the workspace—pause until a human approves.

Daily report digests

Every agent reports completed work, evidence, open questions, and next steps to its own human supervisor.
