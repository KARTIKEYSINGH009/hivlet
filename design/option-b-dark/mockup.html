What changes, and what does not:
Changes: light ivory content area, white cards, stone text, stone buttons, and rainbow pills all go. They are replaced by ink page, coal cards, raised task cards, ivory text, muted secondary text, gold primary actions, and muted green or red only for status.
Does not change: the palette, the typography first rule, zero images over mediocre ones, or any feature, route, or data. This is a visual system change, not a feature change. No light mode toggle in this version, because that would keep two systems alive.
